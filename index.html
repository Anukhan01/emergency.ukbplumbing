Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find your answer? Send us an enquiry or call 07359 605766.

What plumbing services do you provide?

We cover a broad range of plumbing work — emergency plumbing, burst and leaking pipes, blocked drains, bathroom plumbing, boiler and heating services, and general repairs. Final service list to be confirmed by UKB Plumbing.

Do you handle emergency plumbing issues?

Yes — if you have an urgent plumbing problem, call us on 07359 605766 and we’ll talk through what’s happening and how we can help. Availability details to be confirmed by UKB Plumbing.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Southampton and cover Romsey, Lymington, Lyndhurst, Brockenhurst, Eastleigh and the surrounding areas. Get in touch with your postcode and we’ll confirm whether we cover you.

How can I request a plumbing quote?

The quickest way is to call 07359 605766 or use the enquiry form on this page. Tell us what you need and we’ll get back to you.

Do you work with residential and commercial properties?

Yes — we work with both homes and businesses. Tell us about your property and requirements and we’ll advise on the right approach.

How can I contact your team?

Call 07359 605766, email info@ukbplumbing.co.uk, or send the enquiry form on this page — whatever suits you best.

Do you provide information about pricing before work begins?

We believe in clear communication. Exact pricing policy to be confirmed by UKB Plumbing — please ask when you enquire and we’ll explain how costs work for your job.
