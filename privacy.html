Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

UKB Plumbing Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry form or contact us by phone or email, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address and a description of your enquiry. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and to provide the services you request.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To arrange and carry out plumbing work you have asked for.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information we no longer need. To do so, contact us at info@ukbplumbing.co.uk.
